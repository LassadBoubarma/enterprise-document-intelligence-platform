# People Data Access Policy — Synthetic Demo

Document ID: HR-001

## Scope
This policy covers employee personal data, compensation data and performance information.

## Compensation records
Individual employee compensation records may be accessed only by authorised HR staff and specifically approved payroll administrators. Line managers may receive compensation information for their direct reports only through approved HR workflows.

Engineering, Security and Risk staff do not receive access to employee compensation records solely because of their technical or control roles.

## Data handling
Exports containing compensation data must be stored only in approved HR locations and deleted when the approved business purpose ends.

## Audit
Access to compensation datasets is logged and reviewed by the HR data owner.
