# Enterprise Security Policy — Synthetic Demo

Document ID: SEC-001

## Purpose
This synthetic policy defines minimum controls for security exceptions, third-party access and sensitive-system changes.

## Security exceptions
A security exception must have a named business owner, a documented technical justification, an expiry date and a compensating control. Exceptions involving third-party access must also include a supplier risk review. The Security team approves the technical control; the Risk team records the accepted residual risk.

No exception may be open-ended. The normal maximum duration is 90 days. A renewal requires a new review rather than an automatic extension.

## Third-party access
Third-party identities must be individually attributable. Shared accounts are prohibited for privileged access. Access must be time-bounded and removed at the end of the approved engagement.

## Audit evidence
Approval, expiry date, owner, affected system and compensating control must be retained in the exception record.
